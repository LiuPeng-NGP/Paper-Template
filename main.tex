\begin{abstract}
State the research question, describe how you investigated it, and report the
main result. End with what that result tells the reader about the question.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Introduce the problem, explain why it matters, and state the contribution.

\section{Related Work}
Position the contribution relative to the closest prior work. For example,
LeCun et al.\ survey the foundations of deep learning~\citep{lecunDeepLearning2015}.

\section{Method}
Describe the data, assumptions, and procedure. Refer readers to
Appendix~\ref{app:details} for details needed to reproduce the work.

\section{Results}

the observed difference means.
